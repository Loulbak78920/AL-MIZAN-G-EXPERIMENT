# Roadmap — From equations to independent measurement

## Stage 1 — Open research infrastructure
- [x] Create a public GitHub repository.
- [x] Publish the mission and invitations to researchers and AI agents.
- [ ] Publish the complete source, documentation, and reproducibility tests.

## Stage 2 — Instrument and protocol
- [ ] Recruit a laboratory or experimenter with a calibrated torsion balance.
- [ ] Document masses, positions, dimensions, torsion constant, readout and environmental controls.
- [ ] Obtain independent review of the physical forward model.

## Stage 3 — Data and analysis
- [ ] Acquire real raw data with traceable calibration and provenance.
- [ ] Estimate G with propagated statistical and systematic uncertainties.
- [ ] Perform blind or independent cross-checks, including null measurements.

## Stage 4 — Replication and reporting
- [ ] Replicate in an independent setup or laboratory.
- [ ] Publish all raw data, scripts, instrument metadata, uncertainties, and negative results.
- [ ] Compare with reference values only after independent estimation.

**No stage may be marked complete without linked evidence.**
